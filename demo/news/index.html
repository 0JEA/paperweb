paperweb · showcase

A news site made of paper

Four directions, twenty components each. Every sheet is a live WebGL2 simulation — cockle relief, formation grain, contact-hardened cast shadows, torn deckle edges, Kubelka-Munk ink. Nothing here is a texture image.

The text stays real, selectable DOM. Move your cursor across the surfaces marked light: pointer and the light rakes over the buckling.

Reading these

Each page is self-contained and imports paperweb from source — no build step, no external assets, no webfonts. View source on any component and it is copy-pasteable.

Rendered on a real GPU. paperweb's relief texture comes partly from how the shader compiler quantises the noise hash, which software rasterisers do not reproduce, so these will look flatter on a machine without hardware WebGL2.
