Presets

The nine presets shipped with paperlab, loaded unchanged. Text sits on top of the surface, so it is selectable and reaches assistive technology normally.

Scale

The relief is denominated in millimetres at a nominal DPI, so the same preset at a different dpi gives physically larger or smaller cockle rather than a scaled bitmap.

dpi 48 / coarse relief

Low nominal DPI

A 30 mm cockle wavelength occupies fewer pixels, so the buckling reads large and slow across the sheet.

dpi 96 / default

CSS nominal DPI

One CSS pixel per 1/96 in. This is the default, and it puts paperlab's sourced millimetre values at sensible on-screen sizes.

dpi 200 / fine relief

High nominal DPI

The same physical sheet seen closer up: more cockle periods across the element, finer formation grain.

Ink in the sheet

The default mode puts the paper behind live DOM. content:"rasterize" instead snapshots the element and feeds it through Kubelka-Munk, so the text is optically inside the sheet: thin antialiased edges let the lit, textured paper show through, and with granulation on, pigment pools in the relief valleys. Compare the two.

content: behind (default)

Text on top

Real DOM. Selectable, searchable, and unaffected by the surface underneath it. The paper is lit and textured but the ink is flat.

content: rasterize

Text inside

Rasterised and composited through Kubelka-Munk with granulation. The ink takes the paper's shading and the sheet shows through the letterforms.

Layout modes

The cast shadow needs void to fall on. overhang decides where that room comes from, which matters because a canvas that sticks out past its element contributes to the page's horizontal scroll.
